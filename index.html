Fourfall privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Fourfall is a puzzle game for Android made by Romain Castagna. This policy explains what the app does with information about you. The short version: it collects nothing.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps a few things on your phone only, in its private storage:

  • your settings (block style, mode, board size, camera angle, colours, sound, shake),
  • your best score for each setup, and your stars on puzzles, levels and daily puzzles,
  • whether you bought Fourfall Full.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it. Android's own backup may copy app storage to your Google account if you have backup turned on; that is a feature of Android that we cannot see or access.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
  • The game does not contact our servers; we don't run any.

Purchases

Fourfall Full is sold through Google Play. When you buy or restore it, Google Play handles the payment and your Google account details under Google's Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details. Google tells the app only whether the purchase exists, so it can unlock the content.

Permissions

  • Vibration: a short buzz when a piece lands and when a layer clears.
  • Internet / network state: required by Google Play Billing to check purchases. The game itself loads nothing from the internet.

While Shake is on in the pause menu, the game reads the phone's motion sensor to notice a sideways shake. The readings are used on the spot to move the piece and are never stored or sent anywhere. Android asks no permission for this.

Children

Fourfall is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
